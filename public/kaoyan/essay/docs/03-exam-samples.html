英语大作文 · 真题套用示范

5 篇完整范文 + 1 个双图表首段｜图表与数据全部取自真题原件（上级目录《考研英语 大作文+小作文历年真题合集 2010—2025》）

用法：不要背这 5 篇。每篇只看「骨架哪些没变、只换了哪些空」，然后拿 03 原件的打卡表去套下一篇真题。 每篇都严格使用 01_最终定稿模板.md 的 10 句骨架（P1 3 + P2 4 + P3 3），P2/P3 只有填空词不同；词数全部压在 150—160，不写超。

示范 1｜2023 真题 · 动态折线（模块 A）

原题：2012—2021 年我国居民健康素养水平。数据：8.80%（2012）、…、11.58%（2016）、…、25.40%（2021）。要求 describe and interpret + give comments，约 150 词。

The line chart illustrates changes in the health literacy level of Chinese residents from 2012 to 2021. Overall, the figure maintained a steady upward trajectory, climbing from about 8.8% in 2012 to 11.58% in 2016 and then surging to around 25.4% in 2021. This trend clearly reflects the growing public attention to health education and disease prevention.

Several factors may account for this trend. For one thing, the growing supply of health information online has made medical knowledge more accessible. For another, with living standards improving, residents are increasingly willing to spend time on exercise and check-ups. For instance, many communities now offer free health lectures, which has further encouraged healthier habits.

In my view, this trend is largely positive. It is important for individuals to turn knowledge into action, while the government should improve public health services. Only in this way can we turn rising health literacy into real health outcomes.

拆解：10 个年份只写 3 个数据（起点 / 中间点 / 终点），而且压进同一句；trajectory、surged 是 Pro 版原句；原因走「信息供给 → 技术便利」+「生活水平 → 经济收入」；P3 用积极语气，展望句把「健康素养」具体化成 real health outcomes，没喊口号。

示范 2｜2025 真题 · 静态多选柱状（模块 B2）★ 最该练

原题：某社区老年人主要日常休闲活动调查。看电视 90.8%、散步 68.3%、养花 34.7%、阅读 31.8%、下棋 18.4%。约 150 词。

The bar chart illustrates the daily leisure activities of elderly residents in one community. Watching television ranks first at 90.8%, followed by taking walks at 68.3%, tending flowers at 34.7%, reading at 31.8% and playing chess at 18.4%. This distribution clearly suggests that most elderly people prefer light, familiar activities at home.

Several factors may account for this pattern. For one thing, limited mobility and few nearby facilities have made indoor activities more accessible. For another, with family members away from home, many elderly people are increasingly willing to choose solitary activities. For instance, a short walk after dinner has become a daily routine for many retirees, which has further reduced their interest in group activities.

In my view, this pattern is understandable but deserves attention. It is important for families to visit the elderly more often, while the community should offer more group activities. Only in this way can we help older residents lead a richer social life.

拆解：柱子但没有时间轴 → 现在时、ranks first、不用 from...to...；各项加起来远超 100%（多选题），所以没有写 The remaining X%；五类全列在同一个句子里——旧稿只报 90.8 / 68.3 / 18.4，中间的养花 34.7、阅读 31.8 会凭空消失；P2 主题句换成 this pattern，P3 观点句也跟着换 this pattern（这是静态图最容易漏的一致性问题）；建议落到 families / community 两个具体主体。

把这篇和 01 原件的「静态图」模板对照看：原 04 的静态模板用 accounts for the largest share + The remaining X%，适合饼图；用到 2024/2025 这种多选柱状上会写错。这就是 B1 / B2 必须分开的原因。

示范 3｜2020 真题 · 饼图（模块 B1）

原题：某高校学生手机阅读目的调查。学习知识 59.5%、消磨时间 21.3%、获取信息 17.0%、其他 2.2%。约 150 词。

The pie chart illustrates the main purposes of mobile reading among college students. Acquiring knowledge accounts for the largest share at 59.5%, followed by killing time at 21.3%, obtaining information at 17.0% and other purposes at 2.2%. This distribution clearly suggests that mobile reading is mainly used for learning rather than for pure entertainment.

Several factors may account for this pattern. For one thing, the growing supply of e-books and academic apps has made learning materials more accessible. For another, with academic pressure increasing, students are increasingly willing to use fragmented time for study. For instance, many students now read articles between classes, which has further blurred the line between study and leisure.

In my view, this pattern is largely positive. It is important for individuals to limit passive screen time, while universities should offer better digital resources. Only in this way can we make mobile reading a genuine aid to learning.

拆解：4 个扇区刚好写满「最大 / 次大 / 第三 / 其他」，因为总和 = 100%，B1 才允许 accounts for the largest share 和 The remaining ...；主题词直接取自《英语历年主题词汇总及拓展》2020 年条目（acquire knowledge / obtain information / kill time）。

示范 4｜2012 真题 · 表格特例（模块 C）

原题：某公司员工工作满意度调查。≤40 岁：满意 16.7% / 不清楚 50.0% / 不满意 33.3%；41—50 岁：0.0% / 36.0% / 64.0%；>50 岁：40.0% / 50.0% / 10.0%。至少 150 词。

The table compares job satisfaction among different age groups in a company. The most striking feature is that employees aged 41–50 reported the highest dissatisfaction, at 64.0%, while none of them chose "satisfied". By contrast, 40.0% of those over 50 were satisfied, the highest of the three groups, which suggests that job satisfaction varies considerably with age.

Several factors may account for this situation. For one thing, the pressure of mid-career competition has made work less satisfying for employees in their forties. For another, with senior staff nearing retirement, they are increasingly willing to value a stable environment. For instance, many firms now offer flexible schedules to senior staff, which has further improved their satisfaction.

In my view, this situation deserves attention. It is important for employers to offer clearer career paths, while individuals should maintain a healthy work-life balance. Only in this way can we ease the mid-career pressure shown in the table.

拆解：这是 27 版讲义点名的唯一特例——表格毫无趋势，不能写 rose / declined。P1 改成「最大差异 + 反向对比」；P2 主题句用 this situation，P3 观点句也跟着用 this situation；主题词取自 2012 年条目（job satisfaction / work stress / employee benefits）。

示范 5｜2024 真题 · 静态多选柱状（模块 B2，换词练习）

原题：某高校劳动实践课学生主要收获调查。学习相关知识 91.3%、提升动手能力 84.8%、感觉心情舒畅 54.4%、增强合作能力 32.6%。约 150 词。

The bar chart illustrates the main gains reported by students taking labor practice courses at one university. Acquiring relevant knowledge ranks first at 91.3%, followed by improved practical skills at 84.8%, feeling relaxed at 54.4% and better cooperation at 32.6%. This distribution clearly suggests that students value practical gains far more than cooperation skills.

Several factors may account for this pattern. For one thing, courses combining theory with real tasks have made practical skills easier to acquire. For another, with employers valuing hands-on experience, students are increasingly willing to take these courses seriously. For instance, many universities now require a real project before the term ends, which has further linked classroom learning to work.

In my view, this pattern is largely positive. It is important for schools to add more group tasks, while students should make fuller use of these opportunities. Only in this way can we balance practical skills and cooperation.

拆解：和示范 2 只换了【】里的词，10 句骨架一个没动。把这两篇并排抄一遍，你就能体会「一套骨架 + 只换主题词」是什么意思。点题句故意写成 more for skills than for teamwork，为 P3 的「加团体任务」建议埋了逻辑，比空泛的「劳动实践很重要」更像评论。

示范 6｜双图表首段（模块 D，只练 P1）

27 版讲义 P16—17 用 2026 英一「养老机器人」双图表举例。英语若考到，只换 P1，P2/P3 原样照抄：

The chart on the left illustrates 【左图主题，如 consumers' attitudes towards elderly-care robots】, while the chart on the right presents 【右图主题，如 their major concerns about such robots】.
Overall, 【左图主要信息 + 数据】, whereas 【右图主要信息 + 数据】.
Taken together, the two charts clearly reflect 【共同点题，如 both the promise and the limits of technology in elderly care】.

数据请按题目图表填，这里不编造。另两个首句备选见 01 第 4 节 D 模块。27 版原话：「静静 / 动静 / 动动结合不换骨架，只换每幅图内的主题用词」。

套用练习：只换这 8 个空

拿任意一年真题，先在草稿纸上填这张表，再动笔。骨架一个字不改。

# 空 从哪来
1 图表类型词 + 模块（A / B1 / B2 / C / D） 看时间轴
2 【主题】+【范围】+【人群】 图表标题、图例
3 【年份】或【项目名 + 百分比】——静态图要列全部类别 横轴 / 扇区标签
4 时态（过去 / 现在）+ P2 主题句（trend / pattern / situation） 判型表
5 【点题】名词短语 主题词库，别写空话
6 【原因1】【原因2】 万能填词矩阵六类
7 【例子】 生活化，不写名人
8 【建议1】【建议2】【展望】 「谁做什么」词库，展望写具体

建议练习顺序（覆盖 27 版给英语的全部重点）： 2023 折线（A）→ 2020 饼（B1）→ 2025 多选柱（B2）→ 2024 多选柱（B2 换词）→ 2012 表格（C 特例）→ 双图表（D）

每篇套完只改 3 个错（按你 01 原件的精改策略）：时态、主谓一致、拼写。不要把整篇重抄一遍。

数据核对说明

  • 2023（8.80 / 11.58 / 25.40%）、2024（91.3 / 84.8 / 54.4 / 32.6%）、2025（90.8 / 68.3 / 34.7 / 31.8 / 18.4%）、2020（59.5 / 21.3 / 17.0 / 2.2%）、2012（满意度表格）：均来自真题合集扫描原件，与你 01/04 原件里出现的数据一致。
  • 示范文的英文是套用演示，不是官方范文；词数都在 150—160 区间，考场以「写对、不低于 150」为准（低于 150 直接扣分，超过 170 只是不加分）。
  • 其余年份的图表数据请以你手上的真题册为准，别凭记忆填数字。
