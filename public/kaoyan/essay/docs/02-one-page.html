① 3 秒判型（看时间轴，不看图形）

题目里… 选 时态 P2 主题句
有年份 / from...to... A 动态 过去 this trend
只有 % / 占比（加起来 =100%） B1 饼图 现在 this pattern
只有 % / 占比（加起来 >100%，多选柱状） B2 多选柱状 现在 this pattern
行列交叉表格 C 表格 看题目 this situation
两幅图 D 双图表 各图各判 this trend
图画 / 一句话 E 兜底 现在 this phenomenon

2024、2025、2026 连续三年考 B2。 柱子 ≠ 动态。

② 唯一骨架 · 10 句（闭卷默写）

P1  The 【bar chart】 illustrates 【话题】 in / among 【范围】.
    Overall, the figure maintained a steady upward trajectory: 【指标1】 rose from 【A】 to 【B】, while 【指标2】 surged from 【C】 to 【D】.
    This trend clearly reflects 【点题】.

P2  Several factors may account for this trend.
    For one thing, 【原因1】 has made 【主题】 more 【adj】.
    For another, with 【背景 + doing】, 【人群】 are increasingly willing to 【do】.
    For instance, 【例子】, which has further 【结果】.

P3  In my view, this trend is largely 【positive】.
    It is important for individuals to 【建议1】, while the government should 【建议2】.
    Only in this way can we 【具体展望】.

第 2 句是全篇唯一长句，一句话把数据说完。 静态图数类别、动态图写首中尾，绝不只报前两名。

③ 只换 P1：六个模块（都是 3 句）

A 动态：见上方 10 句，原样。

B1 饼图（=100%）

The 【pie chart】 illustrates the composition of 【主题】 among 【人群】.
【项1】 accounts for the largest share at 【A%】, followed by 【项2】 at 【B%】, 【项3】 at 【C%】 and other purposes at 【D%】.
This distribution clearly suggests that 【点题】.

B2 多选柱状（>100%）★ —— 不写 remaining、不写 by contrast only

The 【bar chart】 illustrates the 【views / gains / activities】 of 【人群】.
【项1】 ranks first at 【A%】, followed by 【项2】 at 【B%】, 【项3】 at 【C%】 and 【项4】 at 【D%】.
This distribution clearly suggests that 【点题】.

C 表格（最大差异和点题压一句；项目太多可写 4 句，P2 就省掉例子句）

The table compares 【比较对象】 in 【范围】 in 【年份】.
The most striking feature is that 【最大差异 + 数据】, while 【反向数据】.
By contrast, 【次要对比 + 数据】, which suggests that 【点题】.

D 双图表（3 句）

The chart on the left illustrates 【左图主题】, while the chart on the right presents 【右图主题】.
Overall, 【左图信息 + 数据】, whereas 【右图信息 + 数据】.
Taken together, the two charts clearly reflect 【共同点题】.

E 兜底：As is vividly depicted in the picture, ... / The caption reads, "..." / This picture clearly reflects ...（本来就是 3 句）

④ 数据句 3 个公式（只背这三个）

静态多项：X ranks first at A%, followed by Y at B%, Z at C% and W at D%.
动态单线：the figure rose from A in 【Y1】 to C in 【Y2】 and then surged to B in 【Y3】.
动态双线：X rose from A to B, while Y surged from C to D.

升 rise / climb / surge｜降 fall / drop / decline｜大 sharply / significantly｜小 slightly / steadily

✘ comparing with　✘ raise　✘ occupy 30%　✘ 动态图抄光所有年份　✘ 静态图只写前两名

respectively 本身不算错，但它要求前后数量一一对应，基础一般就别用，每个数字前重复 at 最保险。

⑤ 原因 6 句（选 2 + 1 例子）

The primary reason is that ___ has become more accessible / affordable.
Another factor is the growing awareness of ___.
This is partly driven by the rapid development of ___.
People are increasingly willing to ___ because ___.
Policy support has also played a role: ___.
For many people, ___ is no longer a luxury but a necessity.

填词六类：技术便利 / 经济收入 / 观念意识 / 政策供给 / 压力时间 / 社会文化

⑥ 结尾 4 组（选 1）

语气 观点句
积极 In my view, this trend is largely positive.
中性 In my view, this distribution is understandable but deserves attention.
消极 This phenomenon deserves attention.
辩证 While ___ brings convenience, it also calls for ___.

静态图（B1/B2）把观点句的 this trend 换成 this pattern，表格换 this situation，兜底换 this phenomenon——和 P2 主题句用同一个词。

建议填具体：individuals → shop rationally / limit screen time｜schools → offer more practical courses｜the government → protect consumers' rights / improve public health services

✘ Only in this way can we have a better future.　✔ ...can we enjoy the convenience of e-commerce without exposing ourselves to unnecessary risks.

⑦ 主题词 7 类（每类挑 2—3 个）

教育 acquire knowledge, practical skills｜就业 further study, job satisfaction｜科技 mobile payment, express delivery｜消费 rational consumption, quality of life｜健康 health literacy, healthy lifestyle｜文化 museum visits, leisure activities｜环境 low-carbon lifestyle, sustainable development

⑧ 交卷前 60 秒

□ 3 段 / 10 句 / 150—165 词 / 无标题
□ 没把静态柱状写成 from...to...；时态对
□ P1 三句：总起 + 一句话列全数据 + 点题
□ P2 四句：主题句 + 2 原因 + 1 例子
□ P3 三句：观点 + 2 建议 + 展望
□ 数据、单位、% 抄对；静态图四类五类全列
□ 主谓一致 / 拼写：government, phenomenon, percentage, literacy
□ 展望句不空喊口号；卷面不涂黑

考场 30 min：审题 3 → 提纲 2 → P1 9 → P2 10 → P3 4 → 自查 2
