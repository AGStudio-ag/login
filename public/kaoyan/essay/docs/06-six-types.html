英语大作文 · 六类完整成篇（含混用红线）

15 分｜150—165 词｜10 句（P1 3 + P2 4 + P3 3）｜三段｜不写标题｜与 01 同一套骨架，没有第二套模板

这份回答两个问题：① 英语大作文到底有几种？② 动态图和静态图的模板能混用吗？ 结论先放前面：P2/P3 的 7 句六类一字不改、完全共用；P1 的 4 句必须按类换，一个词都不能串。 本文件把 01 里只有 P1 模块的 D 双图表、E 图画文字补成完整成篇，六类凑齐，每类一篇可直接默写的 150 词全文。

0. 先说结论：不是六套模板，是一套骨架 + 六种第一段

你感觉"种类好多、准备不够"，其实是把三种不同的分类叠在一起了：

分类维度 有几种 要不要分别背模板
图表题型（本文讲的） 6 类：A 动态 / B1 饼图 / B2 多选柱状 / C 表格 / D 双图表 / E 图画文字 只换 P1 的 4 句，P2/P3 完全不用换
文章主题 7 类：教育 / 就业 / 科技 / 消费 / 健康 / 文化 / 环境 不换句子，只换【】里的主题词（见 01 第 6 节填词矩阵）
小作文书信目的 9 类：邀请 / 建议 / 感谢 / 投诉 / 询问 / 通知 / 推荐 / 道歉 / 申请 只换 3 句（目的句 + 2 个细节句），见 04

所以真正的背诵量是：10 句骨架 × 1 套 + 6 种 P1 写法 + 若干填空词。不是 6 套、更不是 6×7 套。

16 年真题分布（01 第 1 节）：100% 是图表，图画/文字一次没考过。 D 双图表（26 英一首考，英语留权重）和 E 兜底都按 10% 精力准备，认得写法即可，不要为它们挤掉 A / B2 的训练时间。近两年（2024、2025）连着考 B2 静态多选柱状，这才是眼下的重心。

判型口诀：看时间轴，不看图形。 柱子 + 年份 = A 动态；柱子 + 百分比 = B2 静态。 一致性口诀：P2 和 P3 用同一个词。 trend / pattern / situation / phenomenon 全篇只能出现一个，混着写像两套模板拼起来的。

2. 混用红线表（直接回答"能不能混用"）

部位 六类能不能共用 说明
P2 第 5—8 句（主题句 + 2 原因 + 1 例子） ✅ 完全共用，一个字不改 这是"一套骨架"的核心，换题只换【】里的主题词
P3 第 8—10 句（观点 + 2 建议 + 展望） ✅ 完全共用，一个字不改 只有观点句里的 trend/pattern/situation 跟着判型换 1 个词
P1 第 1—4 句 ❌ 绝对不能混 六类各写各的，见下表
时态 ❌ 不能混 A/D 动态过去；B1/B2/E 现在；C 看题目
主题词 trend / pattern / situation / phenomenon ❌ 不能混 一篇里只能有一个，且 P2 与 P3 必须一致
数据公式 ❌ 不能混 见右侧"绝对禁用"列

六种最常见的串味，串了就扣分：

✘ 错法 为什么会错 ✔ 改成
静态柱状写 rose from 90.8% to 68.3% 那 5 个数是 5 个选项各自的选中率，不是同一指标随时间变化 ranks first at 90.8%, followed by … at 68.3%
B2 写 The remaining 18.4% is made up of other activities 2024/2025 是多选题，各项加起来远超 100%，不存在"剩余" By contrast, only 18.4% chose playing chess.
B2 写 …accounted for 91.3%, 84.8% and 54.4% respectively respectively 隐含各项构成一个整体 ranks first at 91.3%, followed by … at 84.8%
饼图用 climbing from … to … / 过去时 没有时间轴，写不出"爬升" accounts for the largest share, at 59.5%（现在时）
2012 表格写 satisfaction rose in both groups 该表格是纯横截面，毫无趋势 The most striking feature is that … while …
P2 写 this trend、P3 写 this pattern 通篇两个主题词，阅卷看着像拼了两套模板 两处统一成判型对应的同一个词

一句话记住：能混的是后 7 句，不能混的是前 4 句 + 时态 + 那个主题词。

3. 六类完整成篇（每类一篇，10 句，150—165 词，不写标题）

用法：不要六篇全背。A / B1 / B2 / C 四篇与 03_真题套用示范.md 相同（同一套定稿骨架，放这里是为了一份文件自带六类、不用来回翻）；D / E 是本次补齐的兜底成篇。只看"哪些句子没变"，然后拿任意真题换【】里的词。

【A】动态图 · 2023 真题（健康素养 2012—2021）

判型：有时间轴 → A｜过去时｜this trend｜公式 rose from A to B 数据（真题原件）：8.80%（2012）、11.58%（2016）、25.40%（2021）

The line chart illustrates changes in the health literacy level of Chinese residents from 2012 to 2021. Overall, the figure maintained a steady upward trajectory, climbing from about 8.8% in 2012 to 11.58% in 2016 and then surging to around 25.4% in 2021. This trend clearly reflects the growing public attention to health education and disease prevention.

Several factors may account for this trend. For one thing, the growing supply of health information online has made medical knowledge more accessible. For another, with living standards improving, residents are increasingly willing to spend time on exercise and check-ups. For instance, many communities now offer free health lectures, which has further encouraged healthier habits.

In my view, this trend is largely positive. It is important for individuals to turn knowledge into action, while the government should improve public health services. Only in this way can we turn rising health literacy into real health outcomes.

词数：152｜10 句（3+4+3） ｜ 本篇红线：10 个年份只写首、中、尾 3 个数据，合并成一句；trajectory / surged 保留；不能把 accounts for 或 ranks first 写进来。

【B1】饼图 · 2020 真题（手机阅读目的，总和 = 100%）

判型：百分比且加起来 100% → B1｜现在时｜this pattern｜公式 accounts for …% + The remaining X% 数据（真题原件）：学习知识 59.5%、消磨时间 21.3%、获取信息 17.0%、其他 2.2%

The pie chart illustrates the main purposes of mobile reading among college students. Acquiring knowledge accounts for the largest share at 59.5%, followed by killing time at 21.3%, obtaining information at 17.0% and other purposes at 2.2%. This distribution clearly suggests that mobile reading is mainly used for learning rather than for pure entertainment.

Several factors may account for this pattern. For one thing, the growing supply of e-books and academic apps has made learning materials more accessible. For another, with academic pressure increasing, students are increasingly willing to use fragmented time for study. For instance, many students now read articles between classes, which has further blurred the line between study and leisure.

In my view, this pattern is largely positive. It is important for individuals to limit passive screen time, while universities should provide better digital learning resources. Only in this way can we make mobile reading a genuine aid to learning.

词数：153｜10 句（3+4+3） ｜ 本篇红线：饼图四个扇区加起来正好 100%，所以 accounts for the largest share 和 The remaining 2.2% 只有这里能写；respectively 也只在 B1 合法。全篇现在时，不能出现 rose。

【B2】静态多选柱状 · 2025 真题（老年人休闲活动，总和 > 100%）★ 最该练

判型：百分比但各项独立、加起来远超 100% → B2｜现在时｜this pattern｜公式 ranks first at …% 数据（真题原件）：看电视 90.8%、散步 68.3%、养花 34.7%、阅读 31.8%、下棋 18.4%

The bar chart illustrates the daily leisure activities of elderly residents in one community. Watching television ranks first at 90.8%, followed by taking walks at 68.3%, tending flowers at 34.7%, reading at 31.8% and playing chess at 18.4%. This distribution clearly suggests that most elderly people prefer light, familiar activities at home.

Several factors may account for this pattern. For one thing, limited mobility and few nearby facilities have made indoor activities more accessible. For another, with family members often away from home, many elderly people are increasingly willing to choose solitary activities. For instance, a short walk after dinner has become a daily routine for many retirees, which has further reduced their interest in group activities.

In my view, this pattern is understandable but deserves attention. It is important for families to visit the elderly more often, while the community should offer more group activities. Only in this way can we help older residents lead a richer social life.

词数：160｜10 句（3+4+3） ｜ 本篇红线：柱子 ≠ 动态，不能写 from…to…、不能用过去时；绝对不能写 The remaining X%；五类必须全列——旧稿只写 90.8 / 68.3 / 18.4，中间的 34.7 和 31.8 凭空消失，这是最隐蔽的丢分点。P2 和 P3 都用 this pattern（最容易漏的一致性）。

2024（劳动实践课收获 91.3 / 84.8 / 54.4 / 32.6%）是同一模块、只换填空词，见 03 示范 5。把两篇并排抄一遍，就能体会"一套骨架 + 只换主题词"。

【C】表格 · 2012 真题（工作满意度，唯一特例）

判型：行列交叉 → C｜本表毫无趋势 → 现在时｜this situation｜公式「最大差异 + 反向对比」 数据（真题原件）：≤40 岁 满意 16.7% / 不清楚 50.0% / 不满意 33.3%；41—50 岁 0.0% / 36.0% / 64.0%；>50 岁 40.0% / 50.0% / 10.0%

The table compares job satisfaction among different age groups in a company. The most striking feature is that employees aged 41–50 reported the highest dissatisfaction, at 64.0%, while none of them chose "satisfied". By contrast, 40.0% of those over 50 were satisfied, the highest of the three groups, which suggests that job satisfaction varies considerably with age.

Several factors may account for this situation. For one thing, the pressure of mid-career competition has made work less satisfying for employees in their forties. For another, with senior staff nearing retirement, they are increasingly willing to value a stable environment. For instance, many firms now offer flexible schedules to senior staff, which has further improved their satisfaction.

In my view, this situation deserves attention. It is important for employers to offer clearer career paths, while individuals should maintain a healthy work-life balance. Only in this way can we ease the mid-career pressure shown in the table.

词数：157｜10 句（3+4+3） ｜ 本篇红线：27 版讲义点名的唯一特例——不写 rose / declined；表格题把「反向对比」和「点题」压进同一句（第 3 句末尾的 which suggests），否则 P1 会到 4 句。P2 与 P3 都用 this situation。若表格里带年份、确有变化，才改用过去时（01 第 4 节 C 模块）。

【D】双图表 · 模拟练习（本次补齐成篇）⚠️ 非真题

数据说明：英语 16 年真题从未考过双图表（26 英一首考）。下面这道题是为练骨架自编的模拟题，图中数字是示范用，不是真题数据；考场上按题目实际图表替换，不要凭记忆填。

模拟题面：左图饼图「大学生使用 AI 学习工具的目的」（查资料 46%、练写作 32%、做翻译 14%、其他 8%，合计 100%）；右图柱状「主要顾虑」（担心准确性 71%、依赖性强 58%、隐私 33%，多选 > 100%）。 判型：两幅图 → D｜左静 B1 + 右静 B2，两幅各按各的公式｜this trend（按 01 判型表）｜每图各报 1 个数据

The chart on the left illustrates why college students use AI learning tools, while the chart on the right presents their major concerns. Overall, learning-oriented uses dominate, whereas worries about reliability remain widespread: looking up information accounts for 46%, while 71% worry about accuracy. Taken together, the two charts clearly reflect both the promise and the limits of AI in study.

Several factors may account for this trend. For one thing, the wide availability of AI tools has made information retrieval more efficient. For another, with academic tasks growing more demanding, students are increasingly willing to seek instant help. For instance, many undergraduates now draft outlines with AI before writing, which has further raised concerns about over-reliance.

In my view, this trend is understandable but deserves attention. It is important for students to verify AI outputs, while universities should offer clear guidance on academic integrity. Only in this way can we enjoy the efficiency of AI without weakening independent thinking.

词数：163｜10 句（3+4+3） ｜ 本篇红线：两幅图都要描述，而且必须写在同一句里（第 2 句用 whereas 并列）；左图（=100%）用 accounts for，右图（>100%）用 71% worry about，两者公式不串；P2/P3 原样照抄 A 的 7 句，只换填空词。

另两个首句备选见 01 第 4 节 D 模块。27 版原话：「静静 / 动静 / 动动结合不换骨架，只换每幅图内的主题用词」。

【E】图画 / 文字兜底 · 模拟练习（本次补齐成篇）⚠️ 非真题

数据说明：图画/文字在英语 16 年真题里一次没考过（大纲允许，权重约 10%）。这是自编的图画模拟题，只用来认写法，不必花时间背。

模拟题面：两个学生备考，一个每天背一小张单词表，另一个考前一晚通宵突击；画面文字 "Little by little, one goes far." 判型：图画 → E｜现在时｜this phenomenon｜无数据可写

As is vividly depicted in the picture, two students are preparing for the same exam in very different ways. The caption reads, "Little by little, one goes far." While one reviews a short vocabulary list every day, the other crams through the night before the test. This picture clearly reflects the value of steady effort over last-minute rushes.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. For one thing, regular practice has made knowledge easier to retain and easier to recall under pressure. For another, with exam pressure mounting and results judged by a single score, students are increasingly willing to look for shortcuts. For instance, many candidates now rely on crash courses in the final week, which has further weakened long-term retention.

In my view, this phenomenon deserves attention. It is important for individuals to keep a daily review schedule, while schools should discourage last-minute cramming. Only in this way can we turn steady effort into lasting progress.

词数：156｜11 句（4+4+3）——全篇唯一允许 11 句的题型 ｜ 本篇红线：图画没有数据可报，P1 必须用 While …, … 补一句画面细节才够字数（第 3 句），所以 P1 是 4 句；其余 7 句与 A 完全一致，只把 this trend 换成 this phenomenon。不要硬编数字，也不要为了凑 10 句把画面细节删掉——图画题删了细节就只剩议论。

文字题只换前两句（01 第 4 节 E 模块）：

The saying "【原句】" conveys a simple truth: 【寓意】.
What it emphasizes is that 【展开一句】.

4. 混用辨析 8 题（做完就不用再纠结能不能混）

先自己判，再看答案。每题都只错在一个地方。

# 句子 错在哪 正确写法
1 Watching television rose from 90.8% to 68.3% among the elderly. 把 B2 多选数据当成时间趋势 Watching television ranks first at 90.8%, followed by walking outdoors at 68.3%.
2 The remaining 18.4% is made up of other leisure activities.（2025 数据） B2 用了 B1 的"剩余"公式，各项加起来 >100% By contrast, only 18.4% chose playing chess.
3 Knowledge, skills and cooperation accounted for 91.3%, 84.8% and 54.4% respectively.（2024 数据） respectively 只适用于构成比 Acquiring relevant knowledge ranks first at 91.3%, followed by improved practical skills at 84.8%.
4 P2 …account for this trend. / P3 In my view, this pattern is largely positive. 一篇里两个主题词 按判型统一成一个；静态图统一 this pattern
5 Job satisfaction rose in both groups in 2012. 2012 表格是横截面，无趋势 The most striking feature is that employees aged 41–50 reported the highest dissatisfaction.
6 Acquiring knowledge accounted for the largest share at 59.5%…（2020 饼图） 静态图误用过去时 accounts for the largest share, at 59.5%
7 The pie chart … climbing from 8.80% to 25.40%. 把 A 的趋势公式套到静态图 饼图改写 accounts for …，或确认题目其实是折线图再用 A
8 D 双图表 P2 写 Several factors may account for this situation. 双图表按判型表用 trend，不是 situation Several factors may account for this trend.

判分口径：错 0—1 题 → 可以进真题套用了；错 2—3 题 → 把第 1 节速查表抄一遍；错 ≥4 题 → 先回 02_一页纸速背卡 把 10 句骨架默到闭卷，别急着换题。

5. 这份资料怎么用（不增加背诵量）

  1. 不新增模板。 六篇用的是同一套 10 句骨架，前四篇就是 03 里的定稿范文。背的仍然只有 02 那一页。
  2. 练习顺序（覆盖 27 版给英语的全部重点）：A(2023) → B2(2025) → B2(2024 换词) → B1(2020) → C(2012) → D → E。D/E 各练 1 次认写法即可。
  3. 每次只改 3 类错：时态、主谓一致、拼写。不要把整篇重抄。
  4. 复习节奏 1-2-4-7：当天、第 2 天、第 4 天、第 7 天各默 1 次（01 第 10 节）。
  5. 练法借鉴：把「看模板」和「练词句」分开——先读成篇（本文件第 3 节），再做辨析 8 题（第 4 节），最后合上资料套一篇真题。这比反复通读模板有效。
