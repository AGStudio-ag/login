先回答那个让你晕的问题：只有 1 套模板，不是 4 套。

你最初那几篇「动态图 / 静态图 / 表格 / 兜底」，是同一套 10 句骨架换了第一段而已，看起来像 4 篇。
真正要背的只有：10 句骨架（P2/P3 永远不变）+ 6 种第一段写法（A / B1 / B2 / C / D / E）。其中真正要练的只有 A 动态 和 B2 静态多选柱状；完整成篇不用背，用来对照。

三、你写的这篇：先给个定心丸 161 词 / 结构对

按考场标准，这篇不改大概 9—10 / 15；把下面 4 处改掉，能到 11—12 / 15。你要的 40—50 分，大作文只要 8—10 分就够，所以不需要推翻重写。

✔ 对的地方 段落和句数都清楚；词数 161 在 150—170 安全区；饼图用 accounts for the largest share + The remaining... 完全正确；P2 用了 this pattern；结尾 reduce the financial burden 具体、不空喊。

4 个必须改的地方

你的原句问题改成
In my view, this distribution is understandable... 硬伤 1P2 用 pattern，P3 用 distribution，不一致。阅卷会看成两套模板拼的。 In my view, this pattern is understandable but deserves attention.
On the other hand, parents attach greater importance to..., so they are willing to... 硬伤 2两个独立分句用 so 连，考场最容易主谓出错；也丢了骨架的 with + doing 复杂句。 For another, with parents attaching greater importance to education, many families are increasingly willing to pay for tutoring.
For instance, many families now spend over 20% of their income on education. 硬伤 3丢了 which has further...；编的 20% 和图表里 education 20% 撞车。 For instance, many families now pay for online courses, which has further pushed up education spending.
Individuals should..., and the government should... 硬伤 4语法没错，但丢了背过的 It is important for... while...，白少一个得分句。 It is important for individuals to make a reasonable budget, while the government should stabilize housing prices.

四、改好的版本 只动了 5 处，其余原样

1The pie chart illustrates the composition of household spending among urban residents in a certain city.

2As the data show, food accounts for the largest share, at 35%, followed by housing at 25% and education at 20%.

3The remaining 20% is made up of other expenses.

4This distribution clearly suggests that basic needs still take up more than half of family budgets.

5Several factors may account for this pattern.

6For one thing, rising housing prices have forced families to spend more on rent or mortgage payments.

7For another, with parents attaching greater importance to education, many families are increasingly willing to pay for tutoring and extracurricular activities.

8For instance, many families now pay for online courses and weekend classes, which has further pushed up education spending.

9In my view, this pattern is understandable but deserves attention.

10It is important for individuals to make a reasonable budget, while the government should stabilize housing prices and improve public services.

11Only in this way can we reduce the financial burden on ordinary families.

词数 改后约 160 词，仍在 140—170。注意 这套只能用于「各项合计 = 100%」的饼图或构成比；遇到 2024/2025 那种多选柱状，第 3 句不能写，要换成 By contrast, only X% chose...

结论：作文不是用来拉分的，是用来「不拖后腿」的。你只要做到不跑题、格式对、字数够、语法错误不挡理解，8—10 分是稳的。把省下的时间给阅读。

七、四条保命规则 基础不好就靠这四条

P2 / P3 一个字不改。你这次改了 4 处，3 处出问题——不是你不会写，是临场造句风险太高。背过的句子错误率最低。
例子句不写数字、不写人名。用 many families / many students / many communities，既安全又不会和图里数据撞车。
判型只看时间轴。柱子 + 年份 = 动态；柱子 + 百分比 = 静态。2024—2026 都是静态多选柱状。
写完只查 4 件事：时态、三单/主谓一致、拼写、数据有没有抄错。别整篇重抄。
