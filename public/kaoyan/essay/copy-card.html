一、唯一骨架：9 句，只填 5 个空

P1（2 句）
1. The figures in the 【bar / line / pie chart】 reveal notable 【changes / differences】 concerning 【主题词】.
2. 【图表数据句，见第二节】

P2（5 句，六类题型完全共用，一字不改）
3. 【主题词】 has attracted attention, not least because of its far-reaching implications.
4. At the individual level, it is reflected in the way people 【具体行为】, ultimately contributing to personal development and well-being.
5. National policies on 【相关领域】 likewise focus on empowering individuals to achieve their full potential.
6. Beyond personal advancement, this principle can strengthen communities by 【社会效果】, laying the groundwork for sustainable progress.
7. As this principle takes root across society, it creates a ripple effect that benefits social harmony and long-term prosperity.

P3（2 句，完全共用）
8. In conclusion, a rational consensus has emerged that both individuals and communities benefit immeasurably when 【主题词】 guides action.
9. Promoting this principle is therefore not merely advisable but essential for holistic development.
只有 5 个空：主题词（第 1、3、8 句必须是同一个词）｜图表数据句｜具体行为｜相关领域｜社会效果。后三个从第三节语料表直接抄。

二、第一段数据句：背两个公式

动态图（有年份，过去时）

Between 【Y1】 and 【Y2】, the number / proportion of 【类别1】 rose / fell from about 【A】 to around 【B】, while that of 【类别2】 increased / decreased sharply from roughly 【C】 to approximately 【D】.
没有稳定项就写到这儿；类别名不会写就写 the first category / the second category，别硬拼。

静态图（只有百分比，现在时）

In a survey of 【调查对象】, 【类别1】 accounts for the largest share, at 【A%】, followed by 【类别2】, 【类别3】 and 【类别4】 at 【B%】, 【C%】 and 【D%】 respectively.
多选调查（各项相加 >100%，2021/2024/2025/2026）可把 accounts for the largest share 换成 has the highest percentage。类别全部列全，不跳过中间项；不写 The remaining X%。

五、交卷前自查

  1. 不写标题；3 段；9 句；字数 175—190（绝不低于 150）
  2. 判型：柱子+年份=动态过去时；柱子+百分比=静态现在时
  3. 静态类别全部列全；多选不写 The remaining X%
  4. 主题词在第 1、3、8 句是同一个（最常错）
  5. P2 五句和 P3 两句一字未改；只填了 5 个空
  6. 主谓一致、时态、拼写；书写尽量满、尽量工整
